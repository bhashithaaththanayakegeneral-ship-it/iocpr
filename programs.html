Our programmes

Programmes that prevent, reform and rebuild.

Six focus areas work together across the full journey — from stopping crime before it happens to helping reformed individuals thrive.

Crime Prevention Awareness

Community workshops, school sessions and media campaigns that build awareness of risks and rights — reducing crime by addressing its root causes.

Rehabilitator Support Programs

Education, vocational training and mentorship that equip reformed individuals with the skills and confidence to rebuild their lives.

Youth Protection Programs

Safe spaces, mentoring and opportunity for at-risk young people — steering them away from harm and toward their potential.

Legal Aid & Human Rights Advocacy

Access to justice for those who cannot afford it, and advocacy that defends the rights of the vulnerable and marginalized.

Community Leadership Development

Training local leaders and volunteers to carry prevention and reform deep into their own communities.

Research & Policy Development

Evidence-led research that informs smarter, fairer crime-prevention policy at every level of government.
