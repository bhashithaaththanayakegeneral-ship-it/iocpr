About IOCPR

Building a world where reform replaces punishment.

The International Organization for Crime Prevention and Rehabilitation is a registered NGO founded in 2025, headquartered in Battaramulla, Sri Lanka — working locally, regionally and internationally.

Our story

A response to an escalating global crisis.

Crime fractures families, drains communities and traps individuals in cycles that are hard to escape. IOCPR was founded on a simple conviction: that prevention, rehabilitation and opportunity do more to build safe societies than punishment alone.

We work with marginalized communities, support victims, and help reformed individuals reintegrate into society — building collaborative networks that connect governments, NGOs and citizens around a shared goal.

Our Mission

To prevent crime and rehabilitate offenders through education, advocacy and collaboration — restoring dignity to individuals and safety to communities.

Our Vision

A world without crime, where every person — including those who have erred — has the opportunity to reform, contribute and live with dignity.

Our pillars

Four commitments at the heart of our work

Prevention

Reduce crime through awareness and education.

Reformation

Rehabilitate offenders via education and vocational programmes.

Advocacy

Champion human rights and social justice.

Collaboration

Foster partnerships between governments, NGOs and communities.

Our values

What we stand for

Dignity

We see the person, never only the past. Everyone deserves respect and a path forward.

Justice

We advocate for fair treatment, human rights and access to legal protection for all.

Community

Lasting change is local. We build with communities, not for them.

Evidence

Our programmes are grounded in research and measured by real outcomes.

Compassion

We lead with empathy for victims, offenders and the families caught between.

Integrity

We are transparent and accountable to the people and partners we serve.
