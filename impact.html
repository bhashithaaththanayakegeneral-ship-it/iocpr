Why it matters

Prevention and reform cost less than crime.

Every person who reintegrates successfully means fewer victims, stronger families and a lighter burden on the justice system. Investing in reform is investing in safer communities for everyone.

  • Fewer repeat offences through rehabilitation and support.
  • Safer youth protected from the pathways into crime.
  • Stronger communities led by their own trained champions.
How we measure

Accountability across four dimensions

Reach

People and communities engaged through prevention and outreach.

Reform

Individuals supported through rehabilitation and reintegration.

Rights

Cases supported through legal aid and human-rights advocacy.

Resilience

Local leaders trained to sustain change long term.
